Project overview

This project is focused on building a website for an organisation. Mine is more focused on a The Potter's House Melville Church which is an NPO. In creating the project, we need to create two proposals, a website using HTML, JS and CSS, Content Research and Sourcing, Website structure and Planning and to submit the project. 

Website goals and Objective

(The Potter’s House Christian Fellowship Church, 2026)The reason for the website is to bring people to God through Jesus Christ and to encourage them to come to church. The website needs to give the visitors proper information that is needed for them to know more about the church while they are engaging with the messages and images on the website. 

(The Potter’s House Christian Fellowship Church, 2026)It is for the awareness of the church to grow. It is to encourage new people to attend the church services. It is to give more information about times and location of the services. It is also to post sermons, to post testimonies as well as miracle video of what Jesus can do. It’s to promote all and any church events like revivals and outreach. To also make it easier for visitors to get a hold of the church and to also motivate people be more involved in the daily church activities as well as the discipleship part of things.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):

It’s to check the monthly website visitors. To check how many enquiries were submitted. To check how many enquiries about the church services. To check the number of views on each of the sermons.
Growth in social-media referrals.

Features and Functionality of Website

The website has the following:

A Home Page that’s has a welcoming message, the vision of the church and calls-to-action.

A About Us page which includes information about the church such as the history of the church, the beliefs, the vision and the mission of the church. 

A Visit Us page which states the details of the church like the location, the times of the services, the address, GPS link and contact details.

A sermon section which is the link to the YouTube channel.

The events section which are the upcoming services, the concert, outreach times and dates the youth times and dates.

Social Media pages where the links of the church’s social media pages are provided.


Design and UX

The website should give a feeling of faith, love, hope, warmth and modern church community.

Typography:

For headings we use the Trebuchet MS font. For the body text we will use the Amino font. For the buttons we’ll use the Trebuchet MS font. 

The homepage should have pictures of the church, fellowships and church activities. It should be easy to use. Important options like Home, About, Location, Events and Contact should also be there.
